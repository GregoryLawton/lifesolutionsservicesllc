# Life Solutions Services LLC

**Life Solutions Services LLC** is a multiservice company that brings everyday business and event needs together under one dependable team. Instead of coordinating several vendors, customers deal with a single point of contact for cleaning, supplies, food, products, and events.

## What we offer

| Division | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Janitorial** | Office and commercial cleaning, deep and one-time cleaning, restroom and common-area care, floor care |
| **Wholesale** | Bulk janitorial and facility supplies, food service supplies, volume pricing, custom sourcing |
| **Catering** | Corporate and event catering, drop-off or full service, custom menus |
| **Retail** | Household essentials, gift items, seasonal selections, special requests |
| **Events** | Planning and coordination, setup and breakdown, post-event cleaning |

## Our approach

- **One point of contact** for every division
- **Built around the customer:** recurring or one-time, small or large, scoped to fit the space, schedule, and budget
- **Dependable follow-through:** clear communication and work we stand behind

## About this website

A simple, static website with no build step or dependencies. It uses the brand colors from the company logo: black, gold, and royal blue.

```
index.html      Page content
style.css       Colors, fonts, and layout (brand colors are variables at the top)
script.js       Division tabs and quote form (the form opens the visitor's email app)
img/logo.png    Company logo
favicon/        Browser tab icons
```

**To preview:** open `index.html` in any browser.
**To publish:** upload the whole folder to any web host, or use GitHub Pages.
**To edit:** division descriptions and offerings are listed at the top of `script.js`.
